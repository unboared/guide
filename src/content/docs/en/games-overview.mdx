## The catalog

Unboared offers two categories of games:

- **Quiz & Trivia**: knowledge-based games accessible to everyone, perfect for breaking the ice
- **Action**: more dynamic games that bring extra fun and competition

## How long each game lasts

To plan your event, here is how long one game lasts with the default settings. Each game page gives the details, along with a ready-to-read script to launch the game on the mic.

| Game | Length of one game | Players | Can you change the length? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Music Bingo](/docs/music-bingo) | About 16 min | 2 to 200 | Yes: short (12 min), normal (16 min), long (20 min) |
| [Unblind Test](/docs/unblind-test) | About 5 min (15 songs) | Unlimited | Yes: up to 40 songs (20 min) |
| [P'tit Bac](/docs/petit-bac) | 5 to 10 min | Unlimited | Yes: number of rounds and time per round |
| [UnQuizz](/docs/unquizz) | 5 to 10 min | Unlimited | Yes, depending on the number of questions |
| [GeoLoc](/docs/geoloc) | About 5 min | Unlimited | Yes, depending on the number of cities |
| [Draw Guessr](/docs/draw-guessr) | 5 to 8 min (4 rounds) | 2 to 30 | Yes: 3 to 7 rounds |
| [Estim'](/docs/estim) | 5 to 7 min (10 rounds) | 1 to 30 | Yes: 5 to 15 rounds |
| [Untiercé](/docs/untierce) | 5 to 6 min (3 races) | 1 to 100 | Yes: 3 to 30 races, about 2 min per race |
| [UnPop](/docs/unpop) | About 2 min | 1 to 16 | No |
| [Poly Vroom](/docs/polyvroom) | 12 to 16 min (4 races) | 1 to 8 | No |
| [Gloofy Pop](/docs/gloofy-pop) | 5 to 15 min | Unlimited | Yes |
| [Bomber Kitten](/docs/bomber-kitten) | 3 to 5 min per round | 2 to 20 | Yes: 2, 3 or 4 min, from the remote |
| [FootBoared](/docs/footboared) | 2 to 5 min per match | 2 to 20 | Yes: 1, 3 or 5 min |

## Which game should you pick?

| Situation | Recommended game | Why |
|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| Early in the evening, small crowd | GeoLoc, Estim' or Unblind Test | Accessible, engaging, doesn't need many players |
| Full house | Music Bingo, Unblind Test or Untiercé | Everyone plays at the same time, even with 100 people |
| Group that knows each other | Draw Guessr, Bomber Kitten or Poly Vroom | More competitive, creates interaction and laughs |
| Music-themed night | Unblind Test or Music Bingo | Dozens of playlists, or your own |
| Custom quiz | UnQuizz | Create your own questions |
| Family / multigenerational audience | P'tit Bac, GeoLoc or Draw Guessr | Everyone can participate |
| Match night | FootBoared | A 3-minute match, perfect at half-time |
| Between two games, or as a running thread | Untiercé or UnPop | A race or a game lasts 2 minutes |

<Callout type="tip">
Quiz & Trivia games generally welcome the whole room. Action games are sometimes limited (Poly Vroom: 8 drivers, UnPop: 16 players, Bomber Kitten and FootBoared: 20 players) to keep the experience optimal: rotate the players.
</Callout>
