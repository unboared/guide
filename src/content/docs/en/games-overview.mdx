## The catalog

Unboared offers two categories of games:

- **Quiz & Trivia**: knowledge-based games accessible to everyone, perfect for breaking the ice
- **Action**: more dynamic games that bring extra fun and competition

## Which game should you pick?

| Situation | Recommended game | Why |
|-----------|-----------------|-----|
| Early in the evening, small crowd | GeoLoc or Unblind Test | Accessible, engaging, doesn't need many players |
| Group that knows each other | Bomber Kitten or Gloofy Pop | More competitive, creates interaction |
| Music-themed night | Unblind Test | Dozens of themes available |
| Custom quiz | UnQuizz | Create your own questions |
| Family / multigenerational audience | P'tit Bac or GeoLoc | Everyone can participate |

<Callout type="tip">
Most Quiz & Trivia games support an unlimited number of players. Action games are sometimes limited (Bomber Kitten: 20 max) to keep the experience optimal.
</Callout>
