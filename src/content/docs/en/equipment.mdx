## The essentials

To use Unboared, you need:

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **A computer** | Laptop or desktop, Windows or Mac. With a modern web browser (Chrome recommended). |
| **An HDMI cable** | To connect your computer to the TV or projector. |
| **A TV or screen** | Any size works. The bigger, the more visible and engaging. |
| **An internet connection** | Stable WiFi preferred. Players use their own connection (WiFi or mobile data). |

<Callout type="info">
Players only need their phone and an internet connection. No app to download.
</Callout>

## Recommended (optional)

- **An external speaker**: for game audio, especially during the Blind Test. The computer's built-in speakers are often too quiet for a bar.
- **A microphone**: if you're actively hosting the evening, a mic will help you be heard more easily.

## Recommended browser

We recommend **Google Chrome** on the computer displaying the game on the TV. It offers the best compatibility with Unboared.

<Callout type="warning">
Safari on Mac can sometimes cause audio issues with certain games. Use Chrome for the best experience.
</Callout>

## On the player side

Players simply need:
- A **smartphone** (Android or iPhone)
- An **internet connection** (venue WiFi or mobile data)
- A **web browser** (the default one works fine)

No app, no account, no sign-up required.
