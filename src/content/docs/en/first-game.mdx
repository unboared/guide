## Choose a game

Once logged in on console.unboared.com, the home screen displays all available games. Each game shows its name, an icon, and an estimate of the number of players and duration.

<Callout type="tip">
For your first time, start with **GeoLoc** or **Unblind Test**. They're the most accessible and engaging games — perfect for breaking the ice.
</Callout>

Click on the game of your choice to launch it.

## The game screen

Once a game is launched, the main screen is divided into three areas:

- **On the left**: the QR code and instructions for joining
- **In the center**: the game itself
- **On the right**: the top scores (today's best and all-time records for your venue)

![Game screen with QR code, game and leaderboard](/images/screenshots/02_game-screen-3-columns.png)

## Players join in

Players scan the QR code displayed on the left side of the screen with their phone camera. They land on a page where they:

1. Pick a **username**
2. Choose an **avatar**
3. Tap **Play**

![Phone screen: choosing username and avatar](/images/screenshots/03_player-phone-choose-pseudo-avatar.png)

That's it. No app, no account needed.

<Callout type="info">
If a player comes back later (on the same browser), Unboared recognizes them automatically — they just need to tap one button to rejoin.
</Callout>

## Start the game

As the host, you decide when to start the game. Wait until enough players have connected, then launch the game from the screen.

Each game lasts about **5 minutes** on average — short enough to make people want to try again.

## End of game

At the end of each game, scores are displayed. The top scores are automatically saved to your venue's leaderboard (always visible on the right side of the screen).

## Switch games

To move on to another game, use the **Home button** in the control panel at the bottom right of the screen. This takes you back to the game selection.

<Callout type="warning">
When you switch games, players need to rescan the QR code to reconnect to the new game. Give them a heads-up so the transition goes smoothly.
</Callout>
