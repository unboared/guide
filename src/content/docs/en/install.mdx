## Step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect your computer to the TV">
Plug your computer into the TV (or projector) with an HDMI cable. Make sure the TV is displaying your computer screen.
</Step>

<Step title="Open Chrome">
Launch Google Chrome on your computer. We recommend Chrome for the best compatibility.
</Step>

<Step title="Go to console.unboared.com">
Type `console.unboared.com` in the address bar and sign in to your account.
</Step>

<Step title="Go full screen">
Press **F11** (or **Fn + F** on Mac) to put the browser in full screen mode. This makes the display more immersive and the QR code easier to scan.
</Step>

<Step title="Check the QR code">
The home screen appears with the list of available games. When you launch a game, the QR code will show up so players can join.
</Step>
</Steps>

![Console home screen with available games](/images/screenshots/01_console-home-screen.png)

<Callout type="tip">
Test the QR code with your own phone before your customers arrive. This lets you make sure everything is working properly.
</Callout>

## Audio settings

Sound matters, especially for the Blind Test. Remember to:

1. **Turn up the volume** on your computer
2. **Turn up the volume** on the TV (if audio goes through HDMI)
3. **Click once** inside the game page — browsers sometimes block auto-play audio until there's been an interaction

If you have an external speaker, plug it into the audio output of your computer or TV.
