## Opening "My account"

On the game menu, click **My account**, at the top right. You can also go straight to [console.unboared.com/account](https://console.unboared.com/account).

![The top bar of the game menu: "Help" and "My account" are on the right (shown here in French)](/images/guide/barre-haut.jpg)

You'll rarely need it during an event, but everything administrative lives here. To go back to the game menu, click **Close**, at the top right.

## The five sections

They are listed on the left of the screen. Click a section to open it. Depending on your situation, some may not appear: **My brand** is only shown to the venue's administrator, and **Referral** appears while your subscription or trial is running.

![My account: the five sections on the left, here with "My brand" open (shown here in French)](/images/guide/mon-compte.jpg)

| Section | What you'll find there |
|---|---|
| **Subscription** | Your plan, your card and your invoices (**Manage my subscription (card, invoices)** button). This is also where you switch to yearly or to the Pro plan (**Change plan** block), and where you can pause or cancel. → [Manage my subscription](/docs/manage-subscription) |
| **Venue** | The name shown on the TV, the screen language, and the language setting for players' phones. |
| **My brand** | Your logo and your colours (Pro plan). → [Your Logo and Colours](/docs/my-brand) |
| **Referral** | Your link and your code to give to a fellow venue or host: they get a 1-month free trial instead of 14 days, and you get 1 month free as soon as they subscribe. |
| **My profile** | Your name, your email, your password, and your cookie choices. → [Edit my profile](/docs/profile-settings) |

Under each section name, a short line sums up where you stand: for example your plan and its price under **Subscription**.

<Callout type="info">
Only the venue's **administrator** can change plan.
</Callout>

## Logging out

The **Logout** button sits below the list of sections.
