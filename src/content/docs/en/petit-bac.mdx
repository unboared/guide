## How it works

The concept follows the classic word game: a letter is drawn at random, and players must find words starting with that letter in the displayed categories (city, animal, profession, etc.). Everything happens on their phone, with a time limit.

## How a game plays out

### Setup

Before starting, you can adjust:
- The **number of rounds**
- The **time per round** (in seconds)
- The **categories** used

### During the game

![Player interface on phone — P'tit Bac](/images/screenshots/08b_player-phone-gamepad-petit-bac.jpg)

1. A letter appears on the screen
2. The categories show up on each player's phone
3. Players type their answers as fast as possible
4. A countdown shows the remaining time
5. When time runs out, answers are compared and points awarded

### End of the game

The final leaderboard is displayed. Players who found unique words (that nobody else found) earn bonus points.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's P'tit Bac time! A letter will appear and you need to find a word for each category starting with that letter. Watch out -- original answers score more points!"

**During the game:**
- Comment on tricky letters: "Q! Good luck everyone!"
- Read out some creative answers between rounds
- Point out duplicate answers: "4 people said the same thing!"

<Callout type="tip">
P'tit Bac is a timeless classic that works with any audience. It's especially popular during multigenerational events.
</Callout>
