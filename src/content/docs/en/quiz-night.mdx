## Format

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 1h30 to 2h |
| **Games used** | GeoLoc, Unblind Test, P'tit Bac |
| **Audience** | All audiences, trivia lovers |
| **Ideal for** | Trivia nights, afterworks |

## Recommended schedule

| Timing | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| **0-2 min** | Connection and explanation |
| **2-20 min** | GeoLoc (15 min of play + results) |
| **20-35 min** | Break (15 min) |
| **35-55 min** | Unblind Test (15 min of play + results) |
| **55-70 min** | Break (15 min) |
| **70-85 min** | P'tit Bac (15 min of play + results) |
| **85-90 min** | Final leaderboard and prize ceremony |

<Callout type="tip">
Award a small prize to each game winner, then a bigger prize to the overall winner of the cumulative leaderboard for the night. It keeps motivation high throughout the event.
</Callout>

## Variant with UnQuizz

Replace one of the games with a custom UnQuizz about your venue or your city. It's a great way to create a unique connection with your audience.

<Callout type="warning">
Remember: every time you switch games, players need to rescan the QR code. Give them a heads-up and allow 1-2 minutes for the transition.
</Callout>
