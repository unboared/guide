## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | 2 to 30 |
| **Duration** | 5 to 8 minutes with the default settings (4 rounds) |
| **Customizable** | Yes (theme, difficulty, number of rounds, drawing time, guessing time) |

## How it works

Each player gets a phrase to draw on their phone, different for everyone. Everybody draws at the same time. Then each player receives someone else's drawing and has to guess what it shows. Guessing right scores points, and so does being guessed.

The drawings appear live on the TV while players draw: that is what gets the room laughing.

## Before you start

Click **Settings**, at the top right of the game's home screen, or use the [remote](/docs/screen-controls) on your phone. Choose your settings before you start: they apply when the game begins.

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Mix, Family friendly, Party, Movies & pop culture, Food & cooking, Animals, Jobs & everyday life, Sport & action, Halloween | Mix |
| **Difficulty** | Progressive, Easy, Medium, Hard, All | Progressive |
| **Number of rounds** | 3, 4, 5 or 7 | 4 |
| **Drawing time** | 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds | 60 seconds |
| **Guessing time** | 15, 25 or 40 seconds | 25 seconds |

You need at least **2 players** to start. The game is more fun from 4 or 5.

<Callout type="tip">
**Our advice to get started**: the mixed theme with progressive difficulty. The first rounds are easy, the last one is tougher. For a family crowd, pick the family-friendly theme.
</Callout>

## The game

### A round, step by step

1. **Get ready**: the TV announces the round, players pick up their phones
2. **Drawing**: each player draws their phrase (colours, three brush sizes, eraser, undo). They tap **Done** when finished. Drawings appear live on the TV
3. **Guessing**: each player receives someone else's drawing and types what they think it is. One answer only
4. **Judging**: answers close to the phrase are accepted automatically (typos and accents don't matter). For the others, the person who drew decides on their phone: right or wrong
5. **Reveal**: the TV shows a few drawings from the round, with the original phrase and the answer given. Players can tap 😂 to vote for the drawings that make them laugh

Each step moves on as soon as everyone has finished.

### Points

- Guessing a drawing: 100 points
- Being guessed: 100 points for the person who drew

### End of the game

The podium shows the top three and the **funniest drawing** of the game, the one that got the most 😂. **Play again** takes you back to the game's home screen.

## Your controls during the game

- **Stop** (bottom left of the TV, or on the remote): returns to the game's home screen
- A QR code stays in a corner of the TV: a latecomer can join and will play from the next round

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"Time to draw! You'll each get a phrase on your phone, and you have one minute to draw it. Then you'll get someone else's drawing: it's up to you to guess what it is. No drawing skills needed, quite the opposite!"

**During the game:**
- Comment on the drawings as they appear on the TV: "I have no idea what that is, but it's ambitious!"
- Remind players to tap **Done** so the room isn't kept waiting
- During the reveal, read the phrase and the answer out loud: it is the best moment of the game
- Encourage everyone to tap 😂 to elect the funniest drawing

**Prizes:**
Two possible prizes: the winner on points and the author of the funniest drawing.

<Callout type="tip">
Draw Guessr works very well with groups of friends and tables. With a lot of players, the TV only shows some of the drawings at the reveal: it is best for groups of 4 to 20 people.
</Callout>
