title: Manage my subscription
description: Everything about managing your Unboared subscription, invoices, and cancellation.
---

## Access your account page

Go to [console.unboared.com/account](https://console.unboared.com/account) and log in. You will find all the options related to your subscription there.

## Manage your subscription

From your account page, click on **Manage my subscription**. You will be redirected to your management portal where you can:

- **View your current plan** and the next billing date
- **Update your payment method** (credit card)
- **View and download your invoices**
- **Cancel your subscription**

## Retrieve your invoices

Your invoices are available in the subscription management portal. You can download them as PDF files for your accounting records.

<Callout type="info">
If you cannot access the management portal, try from a different browser. You can also send us an email at contact@unboared.com and we will forward your invoices directly.
</Callout>

## Cancel

The Unboared subscription is **commitment-free**. You can cancel at any time:

1. Go to [console.unboared.com/account](https://console.unboared.com/account)
2. Click on **Manage my subscription**
3. Click on **Cancel subscription**

Your access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

## Reactivate after cancellation

If you want to resubscribe after cancelling (for example at the start of a new season), go to [console.unboared.com](https://console.unboared.com) and follow the resubscription process. Your venue and data will be preserved.

<Callout type="tip">
For seasonal use (campgrounds, beach venues, resorts, etc.), feel free to cancel at the end of the season and resubscribe when the next one starts. It is designed for that.
</Callout>
