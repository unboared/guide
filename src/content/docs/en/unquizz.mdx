## How it works

UnQuizz is a classic interactive quiz: a question appears with multiple answer options, and players select their answer on their phone. The fastest to give the correct answer scores the most points.

The real strength of UnQuizz: you can create your own quizzes with your own questions, perfectly tailored to your context (quiz about your city, your venue, a themed night...).

## How a game plays out

### Setup

- Select an **existing quiz** or create your own
- Adjust the **number of questions** if needed

### During the game

1. A question appears on the TV
2. Answer options show up on each player's phone
3. Players select their answer as fast as possible
4. The correct answer is revealed along with the response rankings
5. The next question begins

### End of the game

The final leaderboard is displayed based on correct answers and speed.

## Creating your own quizzes

You can create custom quizzes to fit any situation:

- **Quiz about your venue**: "What year did our bar open?"
- **Themed quiz**: sports, movies, local culture...
- **Company quiz**: for team building events

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's quiz time! Questions will appear and you need to pick the right answer as fast as you can. The faster you are, the more points you score!"

**During the game:**
- Add context between questions: "The next one is tougher..."
- Comment on results: "Only 3 people got it right!"
- Build suspense when revealing the leaderboard
