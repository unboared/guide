## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Action |
| **Players** | 1 to 16 |
| **Duration** | About 2 minutes per game |
| **Customizable** | No |

## How it works

The phone becomes a fairground gun. Each player moves their phone to move their crosshair on the TV, and taps their screen to shoot. Three waves follow one another: balloons, moving targets, then cans. The best score wins.

## Before you start

There are no settings. Each player simply has to tap **Play!** on their phone: the TV shows "ready" next to their name.

<Callout type="info">
**On iPhone**, the phone asks for permission to use motion when the player taps **Play!**: they need to accept. If they refuse, or if the phone can't do it, the player aims with their finger instead (a link also lets them choose finger aiming).
</Callout>

While waiting, ready players can shoot the curtain to test their aim. Start the game as soon as at least one player is ready.

## The game

### How to play

- **Aim**: turn the phone, like a Wii remote. Small wrist movements are enough
- **Shoot**: tap anywhere on the phone screen
- **Reload**: the magazine holds 8 shots and reloads by itself in one second
- **Crosshair off?** Push it against an edge of the screen and it realigns

### The three waves

| Wave | Duration | What to aim at |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | 30 seconds | Balloons: 10 points each |
| **Target parade** | 35 seconds | Moving targets: 20 points. Watch out for bombs: −100 points |
| **Raining cans** | 30 seconds | Cans: 15 points, the golden can: 100 points. Booby-trapped cans: −100 points |

Hitting several shots in a row multiplies the points: ×2 after 3 hits in a row, ×3 after 6, ×4 after 9. A miss or a bomb resets the streak.

### End of the game

The podium shows the top three and your venue's records. **Play again** returns to the game's home screen: players stay ready, you just start again.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"Welcome to the fairground! Your phone is your gun: move it to aim, tap the screen to shoot. Pop as many balloons and targets as you can, but watch out for the bombs, they cost you 100 points!"

**During the game:**
- Let players shoot the curtain before you start: it is the best way to get the hang of aiming
- Announce each wave: "Wave 2, the targets are moving, and there are bombs!"
- In the last wave, build the tension around the golden can: "100 points in one shot, who's going to get it?"

**Prizes:**
The game is short: play two or three rounds and reward the best score of the night. Your venue's records stay on screen, so challenge the room to beat them.

<Callout type="tip">
The first game is for getting the hang of it: always plan a rematch. UnPop is ideal for small groups, where everyone can easily spot their crosshair on the TV.
</Callout>
