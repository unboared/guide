## By email

The fastest way to reach us:

**contact@unboared.com**

We typically respond **within 24 hours**, Monday through Friday.

## For faster troubleshooting

When contacting us about a technical issue, help us get back to you faster by including:

1. **What's happening**: describe the problem as precisely as possible
2. **Which game** is affected (if applicable)
3. **Which browser** you're using on the computer (Chrome, Safari, Firefox...)
4. **The player's phone type** (iPhone or Android)
5. **How long** the issue has been occurring

<Callout type="tip">
A screenshot or a short video of the problem helps us understand and resolve it much faster.
</Callout>
