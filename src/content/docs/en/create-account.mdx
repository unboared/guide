## Create an account

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to console.unboared.com">
Open your browser and head to [console.unboared.com](https://console.unboared.com). Click **Create an account**.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in your details">
Enter your name, email address, and choose a password. You can also sign up with your Google account.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose your plan">
Select the plan that works for you. The free 14-day trial starts right away with access to all games.
</Step>

<Step title="You're all set!">
Once signed up, you'll go straight to the game selection screen. You can launch your first game right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
The free trial lasts 14 days with full access to all games and features. You can cancel anytime, no commitment required.
</Callout>

## Logging in later

For future sessions, go to [console.unboared.com](https://console.unboared.com) and sign in with your email and password (or Google).

## Forgot your password

On the login screen, click **Forgot password** and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link by email.
