## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | Unlimited |
| **Duration** | ~5 minutes |
| **Customizable** | Yes (number of cities, theme) |

## How it works

A city name appears on the screen. Players must place a cursor on the map on their phone to indicate where they think it is located. The closer the cursor is to the correct position, the more points the player scores.

## How a game plays out

### Setup

Before starting, you can adjust:
- The **number of cities** to guess
- The **geographic theme** (France, Europe, World...)

### During the game

1. The name of a city appears on the TV
2. Each player places their cursor on their phone's map
3. A countdown gives everyone time to think
4. The correct answer is revealed along with each player's distance
5. Points are awarded based on accuracy

### End of the game

The final leaderboard is displayed. Top scores are automatically saved to your venue's records.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"We're going to test your sense of direction! A city will appear and you need to place it on the map. The closer you are, the more points you score."

**During the game:**
- Comment on surprising answers: "Oh, someone placed Paris in Belgium!"
- Celebrate great results: "Amazing, only 12 km away!"
- Build suspense between each round

**In case of a tie:**
Suggest a bonus round as a head-to-head duel between the tied players.

<Callout type="tip">
GeoLoc is the perfect game to kick off an evening. It's accessible to everyone, requires no specific knowledge, and immediately sparks interaction.
</Callout>
