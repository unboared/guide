## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | Unlimited |
| **Duration** | 3 to 13 minutes |
| **Customizable** | Yes (number of songs, music theme) |

## How it works

A song starts playing. Players must recognize the title and artist as quickly as possible by selecting the correct answer from the options displayed on their phone. The fastest player scores the most points.

## How a game plays out

### Setup

Before starting, choose:
- The **music theme**: Pop, Rock, Hip Hop, Electro, French Songs, 80s, 90s, 2000s...
- The **number of songs**: from 5 to 50 per game

### During the game

![Player interface on phone — Unblind Test](/images/screenshots/08a_player-phone-gamepad-unblind-test.jpg)

1. A music clip starts playing
2. Players see multiple options on their phone
3. They select their answer as quickly as possible
4. The correct answer is revealed along with the response rankings
5. The next song begins

### End of the game

The final leaderboard is displayed with each player's score.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's Blind Test time! A song will play and you need to recognize the title as fast as you can. The faster you answer, the more points you get!"

**During the game:**
- Let players hum along and sing -- it builds the atmosphere
- Comment on results between each song
- Mix up your reactions: "That one was easy!" or "Well done to those who got it, that was tricky!"

**In case of a tie:**
Suggest a bonus round of 3 songs between the tied players.

<Callout type="tip">
The Blind Test is the most popular game for a night out. It creates an instant vibe, even with just a few players. Turn up the volume!
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
After several consecutive games, the sound may sometimes stop playing. If this happens, close and restart your browser.
</Callout>
