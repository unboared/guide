## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | Unlimited |
| **Duration** | 1 to 20 minutes depending on the number of songs (the estimated length is shown before you start) |
| **Customizable** | Yes (playlist, what players guess, how they answer, number of songs) |

## How it works

A music clip starts playing. Players answer on their phone as fast as they can. The faster the answer, the more points it earns: up to 250 points for an instant correct answer.

## How a game plays out

### Setup

Before starting, on the game screen or from the [remote](/docs/screen-controls) on your phone, choose:

- **The playlist.** "All-Time Hits" is suggested by default: 250 hits from every era. The catalog has more than 30 playlists, sorted into **International** and **France** and by theme: decades (70s to 2010s), genres (classic rock, country, hip-hop, Britpop, indie rock…), movies and Disney, current hits, and seasonal playlists such as Halloween.
- **What players have to find**: the artist (default), the title, or the work (the movie or TV show) for soundtrack playlists. If you choose "The artist" on a movie playlist, the game automatically asks for the movie instead.
- **How players answer**:
  - **4 choices** (default): the easiest for everyone, 10 seconds per clip
  - **Free answer**: everyone types their answer, small typos are accepted, 20 seconds per clip
  - **Say it**: the player holds a button and says their answer, 20 seconds per clip
- **The number of songs**: 15 by default, up to 40. The estimated length of the game is shown next to it.

### Your own playlists

In **My playlists**, you can create a playlist for the theme of your night:

- **With AI**: describe your playlist in one sentence ("80s for a birthday", "British indie"…). It's ready in about a minute.
- **Song by song**: search for tracks in the Deezer catalog and add them (at least 4 songs).

You can then replace or remove any song. Your playlists are saved to your account and are there for every night. Each game draws songs at random from the playlist.

<Callout type="info">
Playlists are created and edited from the game screen. From the remote, you can choose from your playlists and the catalog.
</Callout>

### During the game

![Player interface on phone — Unblind Test](/images/screenshots/08a_player-phone-gamepad-unblind-test.jpg)

1. A music clip starts playing
2. Players answer on their phone
3. The correct answer is revealed, with everyone's answers and the leaderboard
4. The next song begins

### End of the game

The podium is shown along with the next players. Two buttons are waiting for you: **New game** starts again right away with the same settings and a new draw of songs, **Menu** takes you back to the Blind Test home to change the playlist or settings. Players stay connected either way. To switch to another game, use the **Menu** button at the bottom right of the screen.

Scores reset with each new game.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's Blind Test time! A song will play and you need to find the answer as fast as you can. The faster you answer, the more points you get!"

**During the game:**
- Let players hum along and sing: it builds the atmosphere
- Comment on results between each song
- Mix up your reactions: "That one was easy!" or "Well done to those who got it, that was tricky!"
- Need to make an announcement? **Pause** stops the timer; to cut the music too, use the **Sound** button

**In case of a tie:**
Suggest a bonus round of 3 songs between the tied players.

<Callout type="tip">
The Blind Test is the most popular game for a night out. It creates an instant vibe, even with just a few players. Turn up the volume!
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
No sound? See [Sound and display](/docs/sound-display-issues): most of the time, one click on the game page or the Sound button is enough.
</Callout>
