title: Communication best practices
description: How to promote your Unboared events to attract more people and build customer loyalty.
---

## Create recurring events

The best way to engage your customers is to establish a regular appointment. Pick a fixed day and make your game night a weekly event.

For example: **"Join us every Friday at 8 PM for game night!"**

<Callout type="tip">
Organize tournaments! Over the course of a month, hold qualifiers each week, then a grand finale at the end of the month. It's an excellent way to build loyalty and keep players coming back.
</Callout>

## Invest in physical signage

Table tents, posters at the entrance: make your event visible to everyone walking by. A QR code on a table tent works really well -- people scan it out of curiosity.

## Promote on social media

- **D-2**: announce the event with an eye-catching image or video
- **D-1**: follow up with a reminder
- **During**: post live stories
- **After**: share results, photos, and highlights

## Offer a corporate package

Corporate venue hire is a significant market. Offer a package that includes Unboared entertainment: afterworks, team buildings, client events.

## Bring in influencers

Reach out to local micro-influencers (5k-10k followers) and invite them to an event in exchange for a drink. They'll be happy to share the experience on their channels.

## Encourage sharing

Many players like to take a photo of their score. Encourage them to post on social media, tagging your venue and using your hashtags.

## Feature it on your website

Add a section to your website mentioning your Unboared events. It's an extra selling point for people looking for a venue for their night out.
