## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Action |
| **Players** | 1 to 8 |
| **Duration** | 12 to 16 minutes for a full championship (4 races of 3 laps) |
| **Customizable** | No (each player picks their vehicle) |

## How it works

Players compete in a 4-race championship on 4 tracks: **City**, **Desert**, **Snow** and **Forest**. The TV is split into as many views as there are drivers. Each race awards points based on finishing position, and the best total wins the championship.

Computer-controlled drivers fill the grid: there are always at least 10 cars in the race, even with a single player.

## Before you start

There are no settings. Once your drivers are connected, click the button to start the championship.

<Callout type="info">
From 5 drivers up, each view gets small: use a large screen and seat the players close to the TV.
</Callout>

## The game

### How to play

The phone is held **sideways** (landscape).

- **Steer**: a joystick under the left thumb
- **GO**: the button to accelerate
- **DRIFT**: hold it to slide through corners, release it to fire a mini-turbo
- **STOP**: to brake

A reminder of the controls is shown on the TV at the start of the first race.

### The championship

1. **Vehicle choice**: before each race, every player has 20 seconds to choose on their phone

   | Vehicle | Strength |
   |---|---|
   | **Kart** | The drift king, charges its turbos faster |
   | **Rocket** | The highest top speed, but wide turns |
   | **Sport** | Versatile and lively |
   | **Truck** | A tank, wins every contact |

2. **The race**: 3 laps. Boost pads on the track and mini-turbos make the difference
3. **The results**: race points are added to the standings, then the next race starts by itself
4. After the 4th race, the TV shows the **champion** and the final standings

A QR code is shown during vehicle choice: a latecomer can join the championship between two races.

### Points

Like in Formula 1: 25 points for first place, 18 for second, 15 for third, then 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3 and 2.

### End of the game

After the final standings, **a new championship starts by itself** as long as players are connected. To move on to something else, use the **Menu** button or stop the game from the remote.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"Drivers, take the wheel! Turn your phone sideways. Left thumb to steer, GO to accelerate. In the corners, hold DRIFT and release for a turbo boost. Four races, and whoever has the most points at the end is champion!"

**During the game:**
- Ask each driver to find their view on the TV before the start
- Remind everyone about drifting after the first race: that is what wins
- Announce the standings between races to keep the suspense going
- Recommend the Kart to beginners and the Truck to those who like to barge through

**With more than 8 people:**
Rotate the drivers: one championship per group, then a final between the champions.

<Callout type="tip">
Poly Vroom is the closest thing to a home console game. Groups of friends and regulars love it, and it is fun to watch even when you are not playing.
</Callout>
