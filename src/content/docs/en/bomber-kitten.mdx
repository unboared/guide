|---|---|
| **Category** | Action |

| **Duration** | ~5 minutes per round |

## How it works

Each player controls a character in a top-down arena. The goal: place bombs to eliminate other players while dodging explosions. The last one standing wins the round.

Players move and place bombs from their phone using a virtual joystick and an action button.

![Player interface on phone — Bomber Kitten](/images/screenshots/08c_player-phone-gamepad-bomber-kitten.jpg)

## How a game plays out

### Launch

The game starts right away once players are connected. No setup needed.

### During the game

1. Each player appears in the arena with their avatar
2. Players move using the joystick on their phone
3. They place bombs with the action button
4. Bombs explode in a cross pattern after a few seconds
5. Eliminated players become spectators
6. The last survivor wins the round

### End of the game

The leaderboard is displayed based on rounds won and eliminations.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's Bomber Kitten! You control a character, place bombs, and try to survive. Use the joystick to move and the button to drop a bomb. Last one standing wins!"

**During the game:**
- Comment on eliminations live: "Oh, double elimination!"
- Hype up the final duels: "Only two left! Who's going to crack first?"
- Call out power-ups when they appear

**With more than 20 players:**
Bomber Kitten is limited to 20 simultaneous players. If you have more participants, organize group tournaments: the winners from each group face off in the final.
