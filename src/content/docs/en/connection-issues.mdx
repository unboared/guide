title: Connection Issues
description: Solutions to the most common problems players encounter when connecting.
---

## The QR code doesn't work

**Symptom**: a player scans the QR code but nothing happens or the page won't load.

**Solutions:**
- Make sure your **computer is connected to the internet**
- Ask the player to try using **mobile data** (4G/5G) instead of WiFi
- Check that the player is using the **native camera app** on their phone to scan (not a third-party app)
- On iPhone, make sure the player is **not in private browsing mode**

<Callout type="warning">
Safari in private browsing mode can prevent connecting to Unboared. Ask the player to open the link in a regular tab.
</Callout>

## A player got disconnected

**Symptom**: a player who was connected can no longer play, their phone shows a loading screen.

**Solutions:**
- The player **refreshes the page** in their browser (pull down to refresh or tap the refresh button)
- If they closed their browser, they **rescan the QR code**: on the same phone and browser (not in private browsing), they get their place in the game back

<Callout type="info">
A player who leaves the browser for a moment (to reply to a message, check another app, or because their screen went to sleep) reconnects automatically when they come back to the page. They don't need to do anything.
</Callout>

## The player can't see the game screen

**Symptom**: after scanning the QR code and choosing a username, the player is stuck on a loading screen.

**Solutions:**
- Check the player's **internet connection**
- Try **refreshing the page**
- If the problem persists, **rescan the QR code**

## The entire session is unresponsive

**Symptom**: no player can connect anymore, the main screen appears frozen.

**Solutions:**
1. **Refresh the page** on the computer (F5 or Ctrl+R). The night picks up with the same QR code: players stay connected (the game in progress needs to be restarted)
2. If that doesn't help, **restart the browser** entirely. A new QR code may then appear, and players will need to scan it again

<Callout type="info">
The night keeps going even if the computer loses internet for a few minutes: as long as it's back within 15 minutes, players don't need to rescan.
</Callout>

## Tips to avoid problems

- **Test before the event**: scan the QR code with your own phone 15 minutes ahead of time
- **Stable connection**: a reliable WiFi connection makes all the difference. If your WiFi is unstable, players can use their mobile data
- **Chrome recommended**: on the computer displaying the game, use Google Chrome for the best compatibility
- **Don't close the browser**: while your night is running, keep the Unboared tab open. If something goes wrong, refresh the page (F5) instead: players stay connected
