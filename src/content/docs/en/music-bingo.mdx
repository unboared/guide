## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | 2 to 200 |
| **Duration** | About 12, 16 or 20 minutes depending on the length you pick (16 minutes by default) |
| **Customizable** | Yes (playlist, listening time, pacing, length) |

## How it works

Each player gets a card of 16 artists on their phone, different from their neighbours'. A song plays on the TV: if the artist is on their card, the player marks it. As soon as they have a full line, they press **BINGO**. The game then carries on until the first full card.

The TV checks every bingo by itself: you have nothing to verify.

## Before you start

On the game's home screen, the **Music** card shows the planned playlist. Click **Change** to open the music chooser. You can also set everything from the [remote](/docs/screen-controls) on your phone.

### The playlist

- **The catalog**: the default all-time hits, international hits, decades from the 60s to the 2010s, genres (classic rock, hip-hop, Britpop, country…) and seasonal playlists.
- **My playlists**: the playlists you created in the [Blind Test](/docs/unblind-test) are available here too.
- **Search**: any Deezer playlist with at least 30 tracks.

A playlist needs at least 16 different artists. The game keeps only one song per artist.

### The three settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Listening time** | 15, 20 or 30 seconds per song | 20 seconds |
| **Pacing** | **Automatic** (songs follow on by themselves) or **Manual** (you start each song) | Automatic |
| **Length** | **Short** (about 12 min), **Normal** (about 16 min) or **Long** (about 20 min) | Normal |

<Callout type="tip">
**Our advice to get started**: keep the default settings. Switch to manual pacing if you want to comment on each song on the mic.
</Callout>

## The game

### During the game

1. The TV deals the cards: each phone shows its 16 artists
2. A song starts. Players who have the artist on their card mark it
3. The artist is revealed on the TV, along with how many cards it appears on
4. On to the next song
5. When a player has a line (row, column or diagonal), their **BINGO** button lights up: they press it, the TV checks and shows their card
6. After the first line, the goal becomes the **full card**. The first full card ends the game

A player who forgot to mark an artist that has already played can catch up: the square is highlighted on their card.

### Points

- Marking the right artist while the song plays: 10 to 20 points, depending on speed
- The first line: 50 points
- The full card: 100 points

### False bingo

If a player presses BINGO without a valid line, the TV announces a false bingo and their button is locked for a few seconds. Marking artists that have not played yet is therefore pointless.

### End of the game

The podium shows three awards: **Full card**, **Line** and **Golden ear** (the player who recognized the most artists while listening), then the top 10. **Play again** starts a new game with new cards.

## Your controls during the game

- **Reveal** / **Next song**: move on without waiting for the song to end
- **Replay**: plays the song again
- **Pause**: freezes the game, handy for an announcement
- **Stop**: ends the game

These controls are on the TV and on the remote on your phone (**Next** and **Replay** buttons).

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"Time for Music Bingo! You each have a card of 16 artists on your phone. A song plays: if it's an artist on your card, mark it. As soon as you have a line, press BINGO. And after that, we go for the full card!"

**During the game:**
- Watch the bottom of the TV: it shows how many players are one square away from a line. Build the tension: "Three players are one square from bingo!"
- Get a round of applause for the first line, then keep going: "We carry on, now it's the full card!"
- A false bingo? Tease them gently, the room loves it

**Prizes:**
Two moments are perfect for a prize: the first line and the full card.

<Callout type="tip">
Music Bingo gets the whole room playing, even people who don't know every song: you only need to recognize the artists on your own card. It is a great mid-evening game.
</Callout>
