The Pro plan gives you **all the games of the Standard plan**, plus two things built for hosts and venues that want a night that looks like them.

| | Standard | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| All the games | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Dual screen**: you run the night from your computer, the room never sees menus or settings | Free during the trial | ✓ |
| **Your logo and colours** on the TV and on players' phones | | ✓ |

## What Pro gives you

### Dual screen

The screen the room watches (TV, projector) only shows the welcome screen of your night with a big QR code, the game and your messages. Your controls stay on your computer, in the **control room**: the room never sees menus or settings. You can also run it from your phone.

It is **free during the 14-day trial**: try it before you choose. → [Dual Screen](/docs/dual-screen)

### Your logo and colours

Your logo at the top of the welcome screen and in a corner of the screen during games, your colours on the TV and on players' phones. Games keep their own colours. → [Your Logo and Colours](/docs/my-brand)

## Price

| | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro** | £59 / $79 / €69 per month | £590 / $790 / €690 per year (2 months free) |

No commitment on the monthly plan, just like Standard. The exact price for your plan (and your country) is always shown in **My account** before you confirm.

## Upgrade to Pro

### You already have a subscription

<Steps>
<Step title="Open My account">
Go to [console.unboared.com/account](https://console.unboared.com/account?tab=subscription), **Subscription** section.
</Step>
<Step title="Click « Change plan »">
Pick **Pro, monthly** or **Pro, yearly**, then **Choose**.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Stripe shows you the exact amount, you confirm, done. Your brand shows as soon as the payment goes through.
</Step>
</Steps>

The **Upgrade to Pro** button is also in the **My brand** tab: you can set up your logo and colours first, then upgrade from the same place.

<Callout type="info">
**How much do you pay that day?** Payment is taken right away, but the days you've already paid for this month are deducted. If you're on a yearly plan, you only pay the difference for the months left.
</Callout>

### You're on a trial

When you sign up, pick **Pro · My brand** under "Your plan": your 14-day trial then runs on Pro, with your brand.

If you started your trial on Standard, you can upgrade at any time from **My account**. Note: **the trial then ends and the first payment is taken right away**. Since dual screen is already free during the trial, there's no rush: try it, and set up your brand in the **My brand** tab in the meantime.

### Good to know

- Only the venue's **administrator** can change plan.
- On a **partner rate** (network, negotiated discount)? Email us at [contact@unboared.com](mailto:contact@unboared.com) to go Pro without losing it.
- To **go back to Standard**, email us at [contact@unboared.com](mailto:contact@unboared.com).
