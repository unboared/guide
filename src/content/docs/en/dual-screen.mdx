With dual screen, the screen the room watches (TV, projector) only shows what matters: **the welcome screen of your night** with a big QR code, **the game** and **your messages**. All your controls stay on your computer, in the **control room**: the room never sees menus or settings.

<Callout type="info">
Dual screen comes with the [Pro plan](/docs/pro-plan) and is **free during the 14-day trial**.
</Callout>

## What you need

- **A computer**, ideally with Chrome or Edge: the players' window then opens on the right screen by itself. Other browsers work too: you just drag the window onto the projector yourself.
- **A projector or TV plugged into the computer** (HDMI cable), set to **extend** the display, not mirror it.
- No second screen plugged into the computer? You can run it from your phone (see below).

## Turn on dual screen

<Steps>
<Step title="Plug in the projector and open the console">
Turn on the projector (or TV), plug it into the computer, then open [console.unboared.com](https://console.unboared.com) on the computer.
</Step>
<Step title="Click « Dual screen »">
The button is **at the top right of the console menu**. When the computer sees a second screen, it reads "2nd screen detected" underneath.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose « A screen plugged into this computer »">
When asked "Where do players watch the game?".
</Step>
<Step title="Click « Send the game to the projector »">
The very first time, the button reads **"Allow access to my screens"**: your browser asks permission to use your screens, accept it. The players' window then opens on the projector.
</Step>
<Step title="Click once on the projector screen">
It shows "Click here to go full screen". That click makes the window full screen **and turns the sound on**. Your control room stays on the computer.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
Do the setup once before your guests arrive: the "access to my screens" permission is only asked once; after that, the button reads "Resume dual screen".
</Callout>

## The control room

The control room is the screen only you see. It has three columns.

### Left: the public screen

Three buttons choose what the room sees, with a "What the room sees" preview underneath:

- **Welcome**: your event name, a big QR code and the number of players connected. Perfect while people settle in.
- **Game**: the current game.
- **Message**: your message, full screen.

Just below, you set what's shown:

- **Event name**: shown on the welcome screen, after "Tonight".
- **Scrolling banner**: a line scrolling at the bottom of the screen ("Happy hour until 9pm · Next up: Blind Test"). The switch shows or hides it.
- **Full-screen message**: type your message ("10-minute break, back at 10pm!"), then **Show**. The same button turns into **Remove** to go back to the previous screen.

### Middle: the game

The same controls as the [remote](/docs/screen-controls): change game, set up the round, **Launch**, pause, sound…

When you change game, the welcome screen stays up: **the game only appears in front of the room when you click "Launch"**. So you can get everything ready calmly.

### Right: the room

The players who scanned the QR code, live.

### What about the players' phones?

While the welcome screen or a message is up, phones show the event name, the text of your banner or message, and "The game starts soon…". As soon as you launch the game, they become controllers as usual.

## Run it from your phone

Is the computer the only screen (it's plugged into the TV itself, or it's what the room watches)? It then shows the players' screen, and your phone becomes the control room.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click « Dual screen », then « I run it from my phone »">
On the computer that shows the game.
</Step>
<Step title="Scan the code with your phone">
The control room opens on your phone.
</Step>
<Step title="Click « Use this computer as the players' screen »">
The computer shows the public screen, with no buttons at all. You run everything from your phone.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Leave dual screen

Click **Leave dual screen**, at the top right of the control room. The console goes back to its usual display.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="« Only one screen detected »">
The projector isn't plugged in yet, or it **mirrors** the computer's screen. So the room can't see your control room, it must be a separate screen:

- **Windows**: press **Windows + P**, then choose **Extend**.
- **Mac**: System Settings → Displays → pick the projector → **Separate display**.

Then click "Done".
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="« Your browser blocked the public screen window »">
Your browser blocks pop-ups. Click **Open the players' screen**: it opens in a new tab, which you drag onto the projector. For next time, allow pop-ups for console.unboared.com (icon on the right of the address bar).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The players' window opened on the wrong screen">
Drag it onto the projector, then click inside it once to go full screen.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="« This window is on the projector »">
Your control room is on the projector: the room would see it. Drag this window onto your computer's screen, then click "Done, send the game".
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I closed the public screen by mistake">
In the control room, click **Show the public screen** (at the top): it reopens.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="No sound on the projector">
Click once inside the projector window: that click turns the sound on. Also check that the computer's sound goes out through the HDMI cable (see [Sound & Display](/docs/sound-display-issues)).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I'm offered « See the Pro plan »">
Your trial is over and you're on the Standard plan. Dual screen is part of the [Pro plan](/docs/pro-plan).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
