With the [Pro plan](/docs/pro-plan), your night looks like you:

- **On the TV**: your logo in the top bar of the welcome screen, then in a corner of the screen during games.
- **On players' phones**: your logo and your colours.
- **In [dual screen](/docs/dual-screen)**: the welcome screen of your night uses your logo and colours.

Games keep their own colours: your brand frames them without changing them.

## Set up your brand

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the « My brand » tab">
Go to [console.unboared.com/account](https://console.unboared.com/account?tab=brand), **My brand** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Add your logo">
Click **Choose a logo**. PNG, JPG or SVG, ideally **on a transparent background**: it is shown on a dark background.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick your two colours">
The **main colour** is used for buttons and titles, the **secondary colour** for badges and details. If a colour is too dark to read on the TV, it is adjusted automatically ("Adjusted to … to stay readable on the TV").
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the logo corner during games">
Top right, top left, bottom right or bottom left: choose the corner where it hides nothing important.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview">
Two previews show you the result: **TV home screen** and **During a game**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Everything saves as you go ("Saved" appears). On Pro, the **Show my brand on my screens** switch lets you hide it at any time without losing it, for example for a night hosted by another brand.

<Callout type="tip">
Not on Pro yet? You can still set everything up in **My brand** and see the preview. Your brand will show on your screens as soon as you upgrade, with the **Upgrade to Pro** button in the same tab.
</Callout>

## Tips for a great result

- **A simple logo** reads better from afar: avoid small-print slogans.
- **Transparent background**: a logo on a white rectangle looks like a patch on the dark screen. If you only have a version on white, ask your designer for a transparent one (or look for a "transparent PNG logo" in your files).
- **Logo too heavy?** If the console says "This logo is too heavy", try a simpler or smaller version of the image.
- **Colours**: use those of your sign or your menu, your guests will notice right away.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="My logo doesn't show on the TV">
Check that you're on the Pro plan (the **Subscription** tab shows "Pro plan") and that the **Show my brand on my screens** switch is on. If you've just upgraded, reload the TV page.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I can't see the « Upgrade to Pro » button">
Only the venue's administrator can upgrade to Pro. If your subscription is paused, ending soon or has a pending payment, the console tells you what to do first, in the same place.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I show a sponsor's logo?">
One logo shows at a time. You can put the sponsor's logo up for a night, then switch back to yours. Would several rotating logos be useful to you? Tell us at [contact@unboared.com](mailto:contact@unboared.com).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
