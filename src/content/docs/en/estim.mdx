## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Quiz & Trivia |
| **Players** | 1 to 30 |
| **Duration** | 5 to 7 minutes with the default settings (10 rounds) |
| **Customizable** | Yes (buy or rent, country, region, number of rounds, time per round) |

## How it works

A real property listing appears on the TV: the photos, the town and the type of home. Each player types their price estimate on their phone. The closer the estimate is to the real price, the more points it scores.

## Before you start

The settings are shown directly on the game's home screen. You can also change them from the [remote](/docs/screen-controls) on your phone.

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Listing type** | Purchase price or Rent | Purchase price |
| **Country** | France or United Kingdom | United Kingdom when your console is in English |
| **Region** | The whole country, or one of its regions | The whole country |
| **Number of rounds** | 5, 7, 10 or 15 | 10 |
| **Time per round** | 20, 30 or 45 seconds | 30 seconds |

<Callout type="warning">
Some regions have few listings, and the game may then be shorter than planned. For an event, stay on the whole country, or check your region before your guests arrive.
</Callout>

## The game

### A round, step by step

1. A 3-second countdown, then the listing appears on the TV: a slideshow of photos, the location, the type of home and a small map
2. On their phone, each player can swipe through the photos, look at the map, then type their estimate and confirm. One estimate per round
3. The round ends when everyone has answered, or when time runs out
4. The TV shows all the estimates, then the real price, the **closest** player, the **furthest** player and the ranking

### Points

- Up to 1,000 points per round: you lose 10 points for each percent of difference with the real price (10% off = 900 points)
- A bonus rewards near-perfect estimates

### End of the game

The final results appear with the podium. You can play again or go back to the game's home screen.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"Think you know the property market? Let's find out! A real listing is about to appear: look at the photos and the location, then type the price on your phone. The closest to the real price scores the most points."

**During the game:**
- Read the listing out loud: "A two-bedroom flat in Manchester… what do you reckon?"
- At the reveal, get the room to react to the real price
- Congratulate the closest and tease the furthest: the TV shows both

**To mix things up:**
Play one game on purchase prices, then one on rents. If your crowd is local, pick your region: everybody has an opinion on local prices.

<Callout type="tip">
Estim' requires no special knowledge and gets conversations going easily. It is a good opening game and works very well for after-work events.
</Callout>
