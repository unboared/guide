## Key metrics

On your dashboard, you'll find the key indicators for your activity:

### Number of players
The total number of players who scanned the QR code and played during the selected period. This is your primary engagement indicator.

### Time per player
The average play time per player. A high value (> 5 minutes) indicates strong engagement.

### Usage time
The total time your Unboared installation was active.

## Filtering your data

Use the date selector at the top of the page to display statistics for a specific period:
- Today
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 3 months
- Last 12 months

## Charts

The dashboard offers several visualizations:
- **Player trends**: a chart showing the number of players over the period
- **Breakdown by game**: what percentage of time is spent on each game
- **Concurrent players**: peak number of players at the same time

<Callout type="tip">
Monitor the breakdown by game to identify your audience's favorites. This will help you choose the right games for your next events.
</Callout>
