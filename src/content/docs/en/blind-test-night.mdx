## Format

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 1h to 1h30 |
| **Game used** | Unblind Test |
| **Audience** | All audiences |
| **Ideal for** | Music-themed night, party atmosphere |

## Choosing the theme

Unblind Test offers several music themes:
- Pop, Rock, Hip Hop, Electro
- French Songs
- 80s, 90s, 2000s
- And many more

You can also mix themes throughout the evening for variety.

## Recommended schedule

### Team format (recommended for large groups)

Form teams of 3-5 people. One phone per team.

### Timeline

| Timing | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| **0-5 min** | Rules explanation and connection |
| **5-25 min** | Round 1: 15-20 songs (theme of your choice) |
| **25-35 min** | Break (10 min) |
| **35-55 min** | Round 2: 15-20 songs (different theme) |
| **55-65 min** | Break (10 min) |
| **65-80 min** | Final: 10 songs (mix of the best themes) |
| **80-90 min** | Final leaderboard and prize ceremony |

<Callout type="tip">
The Blind Test is the most popular format for bar nights. Turn up the volume, let people sing along, and don't hesitate to comment between each song. The atmosphere builds naturally.
</Callout>

## Quick variant (30 min)

If you have less time: a single round of 20-30 songs, followed by the leaderboard and prize ceremony.
