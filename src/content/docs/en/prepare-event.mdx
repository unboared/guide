## D-2: Promote the event

- **Announce on your social media**: post 2 days before, then a reminder the day before
- **Display in your venue**: a table tent or a poster mentioning the event
- **Brief your staff**: every team member should be able to say "Scan the QR code on the TV to play!"

## D-Day: Set up (15 min before)

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare your equipment">
Laptop, HDMI cable, TV powered on. Optional: external speaker and microphone.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect and launch">
Plug the laptop into the TV, open Chrome at console.unboared.com, and log in.
</Step>

<Step title="Go full screen">
Press F11 to enter full screen mode. Make sure the display is clearly visible.
</Step>

<Step title="Test the QR code">
Launch a game and scan the QR code with your phone. Make sure everything works.
</Step>

<Step title="Check the sound">
If you're using the Blind Test, turn up the volume and verify the sound works. Click once on the page to enable automatic audio playback.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing games for the night

| Time of the evening | Recommended game | Why |
|---------------------|-----------------|-----|
| Start (small crowd) | GeoLoc or Unblind Test | Accessible, sets the mood |
| Middle (packed house) | P'tit Bac or UnQuizz | Engaging, everyone joins in |
| End (high energy) | Bomber Kitten or Gloofy Pop | Competitive, spectacular |

## Prepare rewards

Have something small ready for the winners:
- A free drink
- A discount voucher
- Some merch from your venue

<Callout type="tip">
Handing out the reward publicly creates a memorable moment and encourages other customers to join in next time.
</Callout>

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Laptop + HDMI cable + TV
- [ ] Chrome installed and up to date
- [ ] Working Unboared account
- [ ] Internet connection tested
- [ ] Sound checked (especially for the Blind Test)
- [ ] QR code tested with your phone
- [ ] Reward ready for the winners
- [ ] Staff briefed
