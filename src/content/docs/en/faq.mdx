## Setup & How It Works

### How long does it take to set up Unboared?

2 minutes. Plug your computer into a TV via HDMI, open Chrome at console.unboared.com, and you're ready to go.

### Is it an app I need to install?

No. Unboared runs entirely in the web browser. There's nothing to download or install — not for you, not for your customers. Just open Chrome, go to console.unboared.com, and you're set.

### What equipment do I need?

A laptop (or desktop) with a modern web browser, an HDMI cable, and a TV. That's it. Optionally, an external speaker for better sound.

### Do my customers need to download anything?

No. Players scan the QR code with their phone camera and play directly in the browser. No app, no account, no sign-up needed.

### Do I need WiFi?

Your computer needs an internet connection (WiFi or Ethernet). Players need internet on their phones (your venue's WiFi or their own mobile data). A stable WiFi connection is recommended for the best experience.

## Games

### How many players can play at the same time?

Most games support unlimited simultaneous players. Some action games like Bomber Kitten are capped at 20 players to keep the experience optimal. For large events (100+ players), the Quiz & Trivia games (GeoLoc, Unblind Test, P'tit Bac, UnQuizz) work best.

### Are the Blind Test songs always the same?

No. The Unblind Test features a large music catalog organized by themes (Pop, Rock, Hip Hop, 80s, French songs...). Songs vary with each game, and you can switch themes to keep things fresh. Your regulars won't hear the same tracks every time.

### Can I get English-only songs for the Blind Test?

Yes. Select international music themes (Pop, Rock, Hip Hop, Electro...) rather than "French songs" to get tracks your English-speaking audience will recognize.

### Can I customize the games?

Yes. You can create your own quizzes with UnQuizz, choose music themes for the Blind Test, adjust the number of rounds in P'tit Bac, and much more. Each customizable game has a settings button at launch.

### Are games available in multiple languages?

Games are currently available in French and English. More languages will be added soon.

### How long is a game round?

About 5 minutes on average. Rounds are intentionally short so players can quickly try again and keep the energy going.

### Can customers switch games on their own?

No, you (the host) control which game is played from the computer. Players only see the current game on their phone.

## Subscription & Pricing

### How much does Unboared cost?

49 EUR per month (taxes included), with no commitment. All games and features are included (statistics, email collection, game customization). You can cancel anytime.

### Is there a commitment?

No, none at all. It's a monthly subscription you can cancel anytime. No minimum contract period.

### How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days to try Unboared for free with full access to all games and features. You'll only be charged after the 14 days if you choose to continue.

### Can I subscribe for just a few months (seasonal use)?

Yes. Since there's no commitment, you can subscribe for your busy season (summer, winter...) and cancel when you don't need it. You can re-subscribe whenever your season starts again.

### How do I cancel?

From your account on [console.unboared.com](https://console.unboared.com), click "Manage my subscription." You'll be taken to your subscription management page where you can cancel in a few clicks. You can also email us at contact@unboared.com.

<Callout type="info">
If the "Manage my subscription" link doesn't open, try a different browser or contact us directly by email. We'll handle your request promptly.
</Callout>

### Where can I find my invoices?

Your invoices are available from your subscription management page (via "Manage my subscription" on console.unboared.com). If you can't access it, email us at contact@unboared.com and we'll send them to you.

### What about music licensing (SACEM) for the Blind Test?

Unboared uses short music excerpts for the Blind Test. As with any music played in a public venue, we recommend checking your obligations with your local music rights organization (SACEM in France, PRS in the UK, etc.). Most venues already have a license for their regular background music.

## Technical Issues

### The QR code isn't working — what should I do?

Check your internet connection, ask the player to try with their mobile data, and make sure they're not in private/incognito browsing mode (especially on Safari/iPhone). See our [troubleshooting guide](/docs/connection-issues) for more details.

### The Blind Test sound stopped working

After several consecutive games, the sound may stop playing. The fix: close and restart your entire browser (not just the tab). Also try clicking anywhere on the game page — browsers sometimes block automatic audio until there's a user interaction.

### A player got disconnected

They need to refresh their browser page or re-scan the QR code. See our [troubleshooting guide](/docs/connection-issues) for details.
