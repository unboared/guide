## Launch the first game

Don't start the game too early. Wait until there's a decent crowd -- **about 30 minutes after opening** is usually the right timing.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose your first game">
GeoLoc or Unblind Test are the best options to start with: accessible and crowd-pleasers.
</Step>

<Step title="Announce the game">
"Attention everyone! We're starting a [game name]! Scan the QR code on the TV to play!"
</Step>

<Step title="Help the first players">
The first ones to scan set the example. Guide them if needed: "Pick a username, choose an avatar, and tap Play."
</Step>

<Step title="Start the game">
Wait until enough players are connected, then launch.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Pacing the evening

- **Switch games every 20-30 minutes** to keep interest high
- **Take 10-15 minute breaks** between games to let people chat and order
- **Mix up game types**: alternate between quiz and action games

## During the games

- **Comment live**: react to scores, encourage players, add friendly banter
- **Get spectators involved**: "Who hasn't played yet? There's still room!"
- **Announce the scores**: "Player X is in the lead with 1,500 points!"

## Switching games

When you want to move to a different game:

1. Click the **Menu** button (house icon) at the bottom right of the screen, or **Change game** on your phone if you're using the [remote](/docs/screen-controls)
2. Choose the new game
3. Announce the switch: "We're moving on to Bomber Kitten! Check your phones!"

<Callout type="info">
Players **don't need to rescan**: they stay connected from one game to the next, and their phone switches to the new game on its own. Between games, the QR code stays on the menu for 10 minutes for newcomers. Don't leave the screen on the menu for more than 15 minutes: after that, players may need to rescan the QR code for the next game.
</Callout>

## Handing out rewards

At the end of each game or at the end of the night:
- Call out the winner by their username
- Hand out the reward publicly
- Congratulate the top 3

<Callout type="tip">
Post a story or a photo with the winner (with their permission). It encourages sharing and makes people want to come back.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

- **A player disconnected?** They refresh the page on their phone, or rescan the QR code if they closed their browser
- **No sound?** Click once on the game page, check the volume, then refresh the page (F5) if needed
- **Screen is frozen?** Refresh the page (F5): players stay connected
- **Nothing works?** Restart the browser entirely (a new QR code appears and players rescan it)

Check our [troubleshooting guide](/docs/connection-issues) for more details.
