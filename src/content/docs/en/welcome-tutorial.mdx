## What is it for?

Right after you sign up, the very first time you land on Unboared, a mini game starts on its own. It's the best way to understand how it works: you experience, with your own phone, what your players will experience.

Allow about 5 minutes. You need the computer you just signed up on, and your phone.

![The first screen of the tutorial, with the QR code in the centre and the "Skip the tutorial" button at the top right (shown here in French)](/images/guide/tutoriel.jpg)

## What it contains

<Steps>
<Step title="You scan the QR code">
The screen shows a large black and white square: that's a **QR code**. Open your phone's camera, point it at the square, and tap the link that appears.
</Step>

<Step title="Your phone becomes the controller">
The screen shows **You're in!** Click **Let's go!**
</Step>

<Step title="You try four mini games">
A quiz, a blind test, a categories game and a short skill game. Each one is very short. The dots at the top left of the screen show how far along you are.
</Step>

<Step title="You arrive in your space">
At the end, click **Discover my Unboared space**: you land on the game menu, ready to launch your first real game.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Short on time? You can skip it

Click **Skip the tutorial**, at the top right of the screen. You go straight to the game menu.

<Callout type="info">
The tutorial is only shown once. Whether you finished it or skipped it, it won't come back the next time you log in. If you close the page partway through, it will start again at your next login.
</Callout>

## Did you sign up on your phone?

The tutorial is played on a big screen. Unboared then offers to email you a link (**Send me the link**): open that email on your computer, and the tutorial will be waiting for you there.

## What to remember

The principle is always the same: **the TV shows the game, phones are the controllers**, and players join by pointing their camera at the QR code. → [Launch Your First Game](/docs/first-game)
