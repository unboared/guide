## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Action |
| **Players** | 2 to 20 |
| **Duration** | 2 to 5 minutes per match (3 minutes, first to 3 goals by default) |
| **Customizable** | Yes (match length, goal limit) |

## How it works

The whole room takes the pitch. Players are split automatically into two teams, one green and one orange. Everyone controls their player from their phone, held sideways like a gamepad. The team that scores the most goals wins.

## Before you start

During the warm-up, players can already run and kick: nothing counts yet. Two settings are shown on the game's home screen, and on the [remote](/docs/screen-controls) on your phone:

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Match length** | 1 to 5 minutes | 3 minutes |
| **Goal limit** | 3, 5, 7 or no limit | First to 3 goals |

Click the kick-off button to start the match.

<Callout type="info">
If you are short of players, bots fill the teams up to 2 against 2. They leave as soon as real players arrive.
</Callout>

## The game

### How to play

The phone is held **sideways** (landscape).

- **Run**: put your thumb on the left half of the screen and slide it
- **Kick**: a short press on the kick button makes a pass, a long press charges a powerful shot. The button lights up and vibrates when the ball is within reach
- **Sprint**: a burst of speed, to use at the right moment (it recharges in a few seconds)

### The match

1. The two teams face off, with the score and the clock at the top of the TV
2. On every goal: a slow-motion replay on the TV, and the scorer picks their celebration on their phone
3. The match stops as soon as a team reaches the goal limit, or when time runs out
4. If it is a draw when time runs out: **golden goal**, the next goal wins

A QR code stays on screen during the match: people can join along the way.

### End of the game

The TV shows the winning team, the **player of the match**, and three rankings: scorers, assists and best defenders. A rematch can be started right away, from the TV or from a player's phone.

## Hosting tips

**At the start:**
"It's match time! Turn your phone sideways. Left thumb to run, kick button to shoot: short press for a pass, long press for a rocket. First to 3 goals!"

**During the game:**
- Commentate like on TV: the scorers' names appear on screen
- Use the slow-motion replay to tease the defence
- An own goal? The TV announces it, so make the most of it

**For an event:**
Run a small tournament: 3-minute matches, winning team stays on. On match nights, play at half-time.

<Callout type="tip">
FootBoared takes ten seconds to understand and can be played without looking at your phone. Up to about ten players, everyone follows their character easily; beyond that, it is a joyful scramble.
</Callout>
