## Communication kit

We provide a communication pack that includes:

- **Unboared logos** in high definition
- **Demo videos** you can use on your social media
- **Event photos** from Unboared events
- **Graphic assets** for your posters and posts

The communication pack is regularly updated. Feel free to come back and download it again to get the latest versions.
</Callout>
