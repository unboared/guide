## Communication kit

Everything you need to announce your game nights on social media — visuals, videos and logos, ready to post. It is free and included in your subscription.

**[⬇ Download the full kit](https://developers.unboared.com/public-assets/kit-communication/kit-communication-unboared.zip)** *(27 MB)*

Or [read the instructions online](https://developers.unboared.com/public-assets/kit-communication/kit-communication-unboared/COMMENT-UTILISER-CE-KIT.html) before downloading.

<Callout type="info">
The visuals and videos carry **French wording**. The logos and the game artwork work in any language. Need an English version? Email us at **contact@unboared.com**.
</Callout>

### What is inside

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **Social media** | 6 visuals and 3 videos, each in vertical (Story, Reel, TikTok) and square (Instagram and Facebook feed) |
| **Game logos** | All 11 games in the catalogue, high definition, transparent background |
| **Unboared logos** | 4 variants depending on your background, in PNG and vector, plus our brand colours |

### The videos

| Video | Length | What it is for |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| [The game night](https://developers.unboared.com/public-assets/kit-communication/kit-communication-unboared/03-reseaux-sociaux/videos/reel-1080x1920/01-la-soiree.mp4) | 16 s | The main one. Post this if you only post one. |
| [Your phone is your controller](https://developers.unboared.com/public-assets/kit-communication/kit-communication-unboared/03-reseaux-sociaux/videos/reel-1080x1920/02-telephone-manette.mp4) | 11 s | Answers your customers' "but how do we play?". |
| [Blind Test](https://developers.unboared.com/public-assets/kit-communication/kit-communication-unboared/03-reseaux-sociaux/videos/reel-1080x1920/03-blind-test.mp4) | 12 s | To announce a music quiz night. |

Square versions sit in the same folder, under `videos/carre-1080x1080`.

<Callout type="tip">
The videos come **without music**, on purpose: Instagram and TikTok push videos that use a sound from their own catalogue much harder, and those sounds are cleared for you to use. Post first, then add a trending sound from the app. The videos are built to be understood with the sound off.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
You will see an **empty frame** at the end of each video and at the bottom of the poster. It is there for you: add your venue name and the day of your game night, using the Text tool in Instagram, TikTok or Canva. For example: "The Corner — every Thursday, 8 pm".
</Callout>

Need a visual that is not here, or a version with your own name on it? Email us at **contact@unboared.com** and we will make it.
