## The concept

Unboared is an interactive gaming solution for public-facing venues: bars, restaurants, hotels, campsites, coworking spaces, events...

The idea is simple:

1. **Connect a computer to a TV** with an HDMI cable
2. **Open Unboared** in your browser
3. **Your customers scan a QR code** displayed on the TV
4. **They play directly from their phone** — no downloads needed

The phone becomes the controller. The TV displays the game. Everything runs through the web browser.

![Unboared in action at a bar](/images/screenshots/09_physical-setup.jpg)

## How players join

A QR code is always visible on the main screen. Players scan it with their phone camera, pick a username and an avatar, then join the game with a single tap.

No app to install, no account to create on the player side. It works on all smartphones, both Android and iPhone.

## What you control

As the host, you stay in control from your computer:

- **Choose a game** from the catalog
- **Start and pause** games
- **Display the QR code** in full screen to invite players
- **Track scores** in real time on the TV

## What your customers see

On the TV:
- The current game in the center
- The QR code to join on the left
- The top scores on the right

![Game screen with 3 zones: QR code, game and scores](/images/screenshots/02_game-screen-3-columns.png)

On their phone:
- A game interface tailored to each game (buzzer, multiple choice, joystick...)

![Player interface on phone — Unblind Test](/images/screenshots/08a_player-phone-gamepad-unblind-test.jpg)
