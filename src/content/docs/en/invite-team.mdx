## Why invite your team?

If several people at your venue need to launch games (waitstaff, managers, hosts), each one can have their own access. No more sharing a single password.

## How to invite a member

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to the dashboard">
Head over to [b2b.unboared.com](https://b2b.unboared.com) and log in with your admin account.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the Members section">
In the left-hand menu, click on **Members**.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Add a member">
Enter the person's email address and choose their role:
- **Admin**: full access to the dashboard, can invite other members
- **Member**: can launch Unboared on the console
</Step>

<Step title="Share the invitation link">
A link is generated automatically. Copy it and send it to your colleague by email or message. They can create their account by following this link.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Members page on the dashboard](/images/screenshots/07_dashboard-members.png)

<Callout type="tip">
The invited member will receive their own credentials. They can launch Unboared completely on their own, without needing your password.
</Callout>

## Manage existing members

From the same Members page, you can:
- **View the list** of all members and their roles
- **Remove a member** by clicking the delete icon next to their name
- **View pending invitations** that have not yet been accepted

<Callout type="info">
To change the role of an existing member or for any questions about team management, contact us at contact@unboared.com.
</Callout>
