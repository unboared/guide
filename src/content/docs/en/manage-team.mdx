## Why add members?

If multiple people at your venue may need to launch Unboared (servers, managers, hosts), you can create a personal account for each of them. Everyone logs in with their own credentials.

## Inviting a new member

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to the dashboard">
Head to [b2b.unboared.com](https://b2b.unboared.com) and log in.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the Members section">
In the left menu, click on **Members**.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a member">
Click **Add a member**, enter the person's email address, and choose their role (Admin or Member).
</Step>

<Step title="Send the invitation link">
An invitation link is generated. Copy it and send it to your colleague. They can create their account by following that link.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Members page in the dashboard](/images/screenshots/07_dashboard-members.png)

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to the dashboard, can invite and remove members |
| **Member** | Can launch Unboared on the console, limited dashboard access |

## Removing a member

In the Members section, click the **Remove** button next to the member in question. This action is irreversible.

<Callout type="info">
Each member can launch Unboared independently with their own credentials. There's no need to share a single account for the entire venue.
</Callout>
